# Weekly Journal 7

## Exploration
This week was all about faces.
Faces. Now that is interesting. I took this one personally. I get told a lot that I seem extroverted. I had to laugh. My close friends know how much of an introvert I am. So this topic made me think of 'the scream' made by Edvard Munch, a Norwegian artist. 
https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Scream-by-Munch
I am many faces, so I wanted to make a sun and moon based face, one with flowers and one with cables. It reminded me of my many sides. The introvert, the extrovert, the social worker, the florist and now a Computer science major. I indeed have many faces, or should we call them facettes. 

My thought pattern is quite fascinating sometimes.
